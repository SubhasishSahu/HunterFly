Qualitative profile

Model A: Full In-House

Core asset
Owned facility + full tooling + payroll
Scaling mechanism
Add technicians & floor space linearly
Time to first revenue
Slowest — full certification + hiring before ops
Key risk
High fixed cost if fleet contracts are delayed or smaller than 100
Best fit given your background
If you want to own the physical MRO business outright

Model B: Asset-Light Hub & Spoke

Core asset
Small certified hub + partner network + shared SOPs
Scaling mechanism
Onboard new certified partners per region
Time to first revenue
Faster — partners may already hold RPTO/quality certs
Key risk
Quality control across partners; margin leakage to revenue share
Best fit given your background
If you want geographic reach without full capex

Model C: Platform-Led Hybrid

Core asset
Traceability/predictive-maintenance software IP + light avionics bay
Scaling mechanism
License software to more MRO operators/DPSUs; drone count is secondary
Time to first revenue
Fastest for software revenue; repair revenue still gated by certs
Key risk
Depends on platform adoption; still needs a credible repair capability to sell trust
Best fit given your background
Strongest fit — leverages your SCN/traceability & data engineering base directly

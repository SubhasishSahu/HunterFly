SEC. 01 — OVERVIEW

Standing Cost & Business Model for a 100-Drone Defence MRO Startup

Currency: INR. All figures are planning-level estimates, not quotes — every hardcoded number is sourced or labeled as an assumption (see comments on cells).

How to use this

Start on Assumptions — every field there is a live input, not a static number. Change one and CapEx, OpEx, Business Models, Breakeven, and Skill Matrix all recalculate immediately, on that page and on every other page you open next. Values save automatically in this browser (localStorage) — nothing is sent to a server, and a different browser or device starts back at the original defaults. Use "Reset to workbook defaults" on the Assumptions page to clear your changes.

Sourcing

Regulatory costs (DGCA/RPTO/Type Certificate related figures) are grounded in published DGCA/RPTO fee ranges (2025-26 sources). Facility, equipment, staffing, and inventory figures are bottom-up planning estimates based on comparable MRO/light-manufacturing setups in Indian defence corridors (UP/TN) — NOT a published benchmark, since no public 'cost to MRO 100 drones' figure exists. Treat every such cell as a starting assumption to replace with vendor quotes.

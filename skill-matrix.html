Reading the gaps: all 4 gap items sit at 'High' or 'Very High' design complexity — RF/comms security, mission software, cybersecurity, and autonomy/AI. This is the same territory the 'Business Models' tab's Model C (platform-led hybrid) is built around: partner or license these rather than hire, until fleet volume justifies a dedicated specialist.
